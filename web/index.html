Continuous Chess

Real-time chess. Both players move at once, moves cost mana, and pieces capture by collision. Create a room and share the code, or take whoever is waiting.

What the civilizations do
Controls

A drag shorter than the threshold selects the piece instead of moving it, which is what makes a very short move hard to issue. Hold Shift — or press Precise on the board, for a touchscreen — to let any distance count and to brighten the hints.

What players can see

Set by whoever opens the room, and applied to both sides. Hidden information is never sent to either client, so it cannot be read out of the connection.

Game settings

Room

Pick a civilization. Your opponent cannot see your choice until the game begins.
